Hi, I'm David Calvert, an engineer working remotely from the south of France.

I've spent over a decade building and running large-scale, business-critical systems, a journey you can follow through my [resume](/resume/). Today, I'm a Senior Staff Engineer at [StrangeBee](https://strangebee.com), where I lead a team while staying hands-on.

On the side, I experiment with many things, including product design, web development and whatever else catches my curiosity. I also read a lot, mostly about tech, Silicon Valley, computing history, leadership and product.

## What I write about

On this blog, I share what I build and learn along the way, mostly about:

- **Observability**: Prometheus, Grafana, SLOs and my [Kubernetes dashboards](/blog/a-set-of-modern-grafana-dashboards-for-kubernetes/).
- **Kubernetes and infrastructure**: running, securing and migrating workloads in production.
- **CI/CD and developer tooling**: making engineers more productive, like [Terraflow](/blog/creating-terraflow-a-cicd-orchestrator-to-scale-terraform/).
- **AI tooling**: putting AI to work for engineering teams, like this [agentic Slackbot](/blog/building-an-agentic-slackbot-with-claude-code/).
- **Leadership and culture**: how teams work and make decisions, like with [RFDs](/blog/building-a-written-culture-with-rfds/).
- **Books**: reading lists and reviews of my [favorite reads](/categories/books/).
